Already have an account? Log in
1
Open the Register page
Go to /users/register/ or click "Sign Up" on the login screen. You'll need a username, an email address, and a password.
2
Verify your email with OTP
After submitting the form, ChatApp sends a 6-digit OTP to your email. Enter it on the verification screen. The code expires quickly, so check your inbox right away (also check spam/junk).
3
Log in
Use your username and password at /users/login/. You'll land on the Messages home page.
4
Start your first chat
On the home page you'll see two panels — Conversations (left) and People (right). Click any name in People to open a chat room.
⏱️
30-minute session timeout
For your security, ChatApp automatically logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. You will see a warning and be sent to the login page. Simply log in again to continue.

Session & Login Security
Automatic timeout, OTP, and staying logged in
⏰
Automatic logout after 30 minutes idle
If you haven't sent a message, loaded a page, or clicked anything for 30 minutes, ChatApp will automatically log you out and redirect you to the login page. You'll see the message: "Your session expired after 30 minutes of inactivity." This is a security feature — it protects your account if you leave your device unattended.
📧
Email OTP on registration
When you create an account, a one-time password (OTP) is sent to your email. You must enter this code to activate your account. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or click Resend OTP on the verification screen.
✅
Activity keeps you logged in
As long as you are actively using the app (typing, navigating, reading messages), your session resets automatically and you stay logged in. The 30-minute timer only counts idle time.
🚪
Always log out on shared devices
On a shared or public computer, always click your username in the navbar → Log Out. Don't rely on the auto-timeout alone.

Add Users
Click + Add User in the Manage Users section. Fill in username, email, and a temporary password. The new user can log in immediately.

Use the Role dropdown (Admin / User) next to each user to promote or demote them. Admins are hidden from normal users and can message everyone. Only the Super Admin can set other admins.

Click the clock icon to toggle a user between Active and Suspended. Suspended users cannot log in.

Click the key icon next to a user to set a new password for them. Useful when a user is locked out.
Message Any User
Click the chat icon next to a user to open a direct chat with them. Admins can send messages to everyone regardless of visibility settings.

Click the delete icon to permanently remove a user and all their data. This action cannot be undone — use with caution.

The Recent Messages table on the dashboard shows all messages decrypted in real time. Click View all → for the full messages log. The icon confirms each message was stored encrypted.

Click Group Chats in the Quick Actions panel to view all groups, their members, and message counts. Admins can delete any group or its messages.

Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions
Why did I get logged out automatically?
ChatApp has a 30-minute idle timeout for security. If you don't perform any action for 30 minutes your session expires and you are redirected to the login page. Just log in again — your messages are all still there.
I didn't receive my OTP email. What do I do?
First, check your spam/junk folder. If it's not there, click Resend OTP on the verification page. Make sure you typed your email address correctly during registration. OTP codes expire quickly, so use the code as soon as you receive it.
Can I search for admin users?
No. Admin and Super Admin accounts are hidden from the People panel and the Search page for regular users. This is by design to protect admin accounts. If an admin wants to contact you, they will message you first and then you can reply.
Are my messages really encrypted?
Yes. Every message is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before being saved to the database. The small 🔒 green lock icon on each message confirms this. Messages are decrypted only when displayed to you in the chat room.
Can I delete a message I sent?
Yes. Hover over any message you sent and a delete icon will appear. Click it to remove that specific message. You can also use the 🗑 button at the top of the chat room to delete the entire conversation.
How do I change the theme / dark mode?
Click the 🎨 palette icon in the top navigation bar. You can choose between Auto (follows your system setting), Light, or Dark mode. Your choice is saved automatically.
I'm an admin — why can't I access the Admin Panel?
Only the Super Admin can grant admin access. If you believe you should have admin access, contact the Super Admin of your ChatApp instance and ask them to set your role to Admin from the Manage Users section.
Can I send files in group chats?
Yes. Files and voice notes work in both direct messages and group chats. Click the paperclip icon in the message input bar of any group room to attach a file.
